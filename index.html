En bref

Dans le Mille marche hors ligne, sans compte et sans pub. Tes joueurs, tes parties et tes enregistrements restent sur ton téléphone.

Depuis la version 1.6.0, pour corriger les bugs plus vite et savoir quoi améliorer, l'app envoie deux choses : des rapports de plantage et des statistiques d'usage anonymes (quels écrans et fonctions servent). Elles ne contiennent ni ton nom, ni celui de tes joueurs, ni tes parties, ni tes enregistrements. Rien n'est vendu ni utilisé pour de la pub. Tu peux couper les statistiques dans Réglages > Confidentialité.

Avant la version 1.6.0, l'app n'envoyait rien sur Internet.

Ce qui reste sur ton téléphone

Noms et avatars des joueurs, parties, fléchettes, statistiques, exercices du Coach, cibles perso, phrases, enregistrements audio et réglages. Tout est dans une base locale. Le micro ne sert que pendant un enregistrement, et les fichiers audio ne quittent pas ton appareil, sauf si tu les envoies toi-même à un autre téléphone avec la fonction de transfert.

English

In short

Bang On works offline, with no account and no ads. Your players, games and recordings stay on your phone.

Since version 1.6.0, to fix bugs faster and know what to improve, the app sends two things: crash reports and anonymous usage statistics (which screens and features get used). They never contain your name, your players' names, your games or your recordings. Nothing is sold or used for advertising. You can turn statistics off in Settings > Privacy.

Before version 1.6.0, the app sent nothing over the Internet.

What stays on your phone

Player names and avatars, games, darts, statistics, Coach drills, custom targets, phrases, audio recordings and settings. Everything lives in a local database. The microphone is only used while you record, and audio files never leave your device unless you send them to another phone yourself with the transfer feature.

What is sent, and why

Crash reports Anonymous usage statistics
Tool Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), EU data region (Frankfurt) PostHog (PostHog, Inc.), PostHog EU Cloud (Frankfurt)
Content the error and where in the code it happened, the last screens visited (not their content), app version, phone model, OS version, language the name of a few actions (for example "game started", "game finished", "Coach drill completed"), game mode, number of players, the screen shown, app and OS version
Identifier a technical installation identifier, not linked to you a random app-specific identifier, renewed every 12 months
Never sent names, games, scores, recordings, typed text, IP address (not stored), location, contacts, advertising identifier same: none of this
Why fix bugs faster, without asking you to report them know which features to improve first
Legal basis legitimate interest: keeping the app stable legitimate interest; anonymous audience measurement exempt from consent under the French data protection authority (CNIL) conditions, with the right to refuse it
Retention 30 days 12 months
Turning it off always on, because it contains no personal data; you can object by writing to the address below Settings > Privacy > Anonymous usage statistics: sending stops at once and anything waiting to be sent is deleted

Offline, these reports wait on the phone and are sent when you're back online. They never slow the app down or stop it from working.

Sentry and PostHog act as processors: they do not use this data for their own purposes. They are US companies, but the data is stored in the European Union; any access from the United States is covered by their data processing agreements (European Commission standard contractual clauses). No data is sold, shared with advertisers or used to track you across apps.

Your rights

The data sent cannot identify you: we cannot find "your" data in these tools. You can turn statistics off at any time, and uninstalling the app deletes everything it stores on your phone. For any question, or to object to crash reports: valentin.drelon@icloud.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
